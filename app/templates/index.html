How the recommendation engine works

STEP 1

We vectorize every recipe

Name, ingredients, cuisine, and category are combined and converted into numerical vectors with TF-IDF.

STEP 2

We measure similarity

Cosine similarity compares a recipe you like against every other recipe in the catalog.

STEP 3

You get the top matches

The five closest recipes are surfaced, along with the ingredients and traits they share.
